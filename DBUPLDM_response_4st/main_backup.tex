{\color{black}
\subsubsection*{\textbf{\underline{Response}}}
We sincerely appreciate the Editor-in-Chief and all reviewers for their constructive feedback and for granting us the opportunity to further refine our manuscript. We have carefully addressed all comments raised in this third round of review. The main revisions are summarized below:}\\

{\color{blue}
1. Enhanced Comparison with State-of-the-Art Methods.

Following Reviewer \#5’s suggestion, we have incorporated additional recent competitive models for imbalanced tabular data classification into our experimental comparison. Specifically, we added DeepSMOTE (2022), LDAM (2019), and Balanced Softmax (2020) as baseline methods. The experimental results demonstrate that DBUPLDM maintains superior or competitive performance across multiple evaluation metrics, further validating the effectiveness of our approach.\\

2. Discussion on Big Data Relevance and Scalability.

In response to Reviewer \#5’s concern regarding the relevance to "Big Data Research," we have added a dedicated subsection discussing how DBUPLDM’s principles can be extended to big data scenarios. We elaborate on potential scalability strategies, including distributed optimization frameworks, mini-batch processing, and kernel approximation techniques that enable our method to handle large-scale datasets.\\

3. Detailed Experimental Setup and Reproducibility.

To improve reproducibility, we have provided comprehensive specifications for all baseline models, including complete network architectures for MLP+Focal Loss, optimization frameworks, hyperparameter tuning procedures, and regularization strategies. These details are now included in both the main text and supplementary materials.\\

4. Expanded Discussion on Limitations and Future Work.

We have substantially enriched the limitations section by discussing computational complexity, potential failure modes, and scenarios where DBUPLDM may not deliver optimal results. We also provide a more nuanced discussion of the method’s applicability boundaries and directions for future research.\\

5. Terminology Consistency and Clarification.

Following Reviewer \#5’s comment, we have carefully reviewed and clarified the usage of "quantile distance" and "composite distance" throughout the manuscript, ensuring consistent terminology and clear distinction between these concepts.\\
